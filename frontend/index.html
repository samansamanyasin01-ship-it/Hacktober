Core Safeguards & Investigative Ethics Architecture

Decision Support Only. Not Evidence. CaseBoard AI assists investigators in organizing timelines and identifying discrepancies. It never delivers verdicts, never predicts guilt, and never calculates culpability scores.

1. Anti-Profiling & Anti-Bias Guarantees

Zero scoring of demographics, race, religion, socioeconomic status, or neighborhood. Analysis is restricted purely to objective temporal and physical claims.

2. Victim Identity Protection

Victim names are masked under a display alias by default to preserve dignity and privacy during cross-agency reviews.

3. Trauma Sensitivity & Sexual Violence Protocol

For sexual violence and acute trauma cases, survivor accounts are never scored for consistency, because neurobiological inconsistency in trauma testimony is standard memory fragmentation. The tool only records faithfully and flags missing physical documentation.

4. Verbatim Anti-Hallucination Gate

Every extracted fact requires an exact character-sequence match in the source statement. Model hallucinations are instantly rejected at the backend gate.
